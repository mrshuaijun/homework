## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-conviction, ultra-contemporary financial interface blending the refined discipline of Swiss typography with high-energy neo-digital accents. It merges the mechanical precision of developer tooling (Linear) with the cultural swagger of modern consumer finance (Cash App) and the structural clarity of premium hardware ecosystems.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for forward-thinking creators, founders, high-frequency digital natives, and modern investors who view capital as an active craft rather than passive storage. The interface must evoke:
- **Instant Confidence:** Uncompromising spatial alignment, crisp geometries, and absolute clarity.
- **Effortless Mastery:** Dynamic transitions, micro-rewards, and minimal interaction friction.
- **Electric Precision:** Laser-focused accents cutting through deep slate surfaces, making critical financial figures immediately visceral.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Language
A synthesis of **Dark-Mode High-Contrast Minimalism**, **Technical Bento Architecture**, and **Specular Glassmorphism**:
- **Bento Framework:** Compartmentalized modules framed with translucent hairline borders, organizing complex data into digestible visual chunks.
- **Luminescent Highlights:** Deep monochromatic obsidian canvases punctuated by high-wattage fluorescent bursts.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Subtle radial mesh underglows beneath translucent frosted panels to communicate hierarchy and priority without surface clutter.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high optical contrast, controlled luminosity, and surgical signal hierarchy.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#08090C`):** An ultra-dense, ink-slate foundation absorbing stray ambient light.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#101217`):** Base bento card container tone.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#181B22`):** Elevated inner wells, interactive modules, and inputs.
- **Surface Translucent (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Frosted glass sheets paired with backdrop filtration.

### Kinetic Accents
- **Primary Electric Lime (`#CCFF00`):** Reserved strictly for focal transaction triggers, positive deltas, affirmative toggles, and critical system states. High visibility on dark foundations.
- **Secondary Vivid Emerald (`#00E599`):** Represents verified states, inward liquidity, positive yield, and frictionless actions.
- **Tertiary Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Applied to synthetic instruments, protocol actions, long-term vaults, and secondary analytics markers.

### Text & Contrast Rules
- **Text High-Emphasis (`#FFFFFF`):** 98% pure white for metrics, headlines, and primary values.
- **Text Medium-Emphasis (`#949DAE`):** Cool slate for secondary labels, table headers, and metadata.
- **Text Diminished (`#525A6C`):** Subdued charcoal for inactive units, hints, and disabled microcopy.
- **Hairline Strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`): Uniform boundary containment on elevated cards.

## Typography

Typography establishes an architectural hierarchy between technical data points and human editorial statements.

### Pairing Rationale
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides sharp technological character, distinct geometric ligatures, and structural authority without feeling clinical.
- **Body & Controls (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean optical rhythm, tall x-height, and neutral precision across microcopy, transactional records, and user inputs.
- **Data & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Used for balances, transaction hashes, ledger identifiers, percentages, and timestamps to guarantee tabular alignment and financial transparency.

### Scaling & Legibility Directives
- Numeric counters, currency symbols, and balances must utilize `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time value shifts.
- Display sizes require negative letter-spacing to form a cohesive typographic block.
- Tiny labels (`label-mono-sm` and `badge-pill`) must stay strictly uppercase with positive tracking to optimize legibility on tinted translucent chips.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial discipline adheres to an **Asymmetric Bento Grid Framework** structured on an 8pt base metric.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout, dynamic column spanning (`col-span-3`, `col-span-6`, `col-span-8`), `2rem` (32px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 2-column or 4-column balanced bento cards, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single vertical stream, cards adapt to full width with micro-wells inside, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) margins.

### Bento Logic
- Information modules prioritize dynamic sizing: primary actions and main balance graphs span major spans (8 columns or full width), whereas ancillary indicators (yield, FX status, security chip) occupy compact companion cells (4 columns or single mobile rows).
- Component interior padding follows strict symmetry: `space-md` for standard cards, expanding to `space-lg` for prominent promotional hero modules.
- Negative space between non-grouped items uses `space-xl` to prevent high-density visual fatigue across large data fields.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth avoids heavy multi-layered shadows. It uses luminous backplanes, frosted transparency, and micro-borders to maintain a sleek, clean profile.

### Surface Stratification
1. **Base Layer:** Matte `#08090C` canvas with occasional, low-opacity ambient color washes (e.g., `#CCFF00` or `#6366F1` radial gradient with 5% opacity, 120px blur) placed off-center behind prominent modules.
2. **Card Substrate:** Translucent acrylic fill (`rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.75)`) layered over a `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus:** Dense charcoal glass (`rgba(24, 27, 34, 0.90)`) with `24px` backdrop blur and an elevated edge highlight.

### The Refined Rim Light
Every card, input, and interactive badge uses a hairline edge to catch light:
- **Default Border:** 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Card Hover Border:** 1px solid `rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.35)` accompanied by a targeted corner bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -6px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.12)`).
- **Active / Focused Border:** 1px solid `#CCFF00` with zero heavy drop-shadow, retaining razor-sharp perimeter definition.

## Shapes

The shape system balances architectural discipline with soft touchpoints. Cards and containers maintain solid structural corners, while buttons, badges, and micro-controls use pill geometry to create immediate tactile affordance.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Bento Modules & Containers:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for interior elements and `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for master card enclosures.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Strictly full-pill (`border-radius: 9999px`) to distinguish actionable controls from inert content modules.
- **Inputs & Data Fields:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to comfortably house monospaced telemetry while matching card contours.
- **Micro Avatars & Tokens:** Circular (`rounded-full`) embedded with 1px border separation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Electric Neon):** Solid `#CCFF00` fill, `#08090C` high-contrast bold typography, pill-shaped (`9999px`). Hover triggers a subtle scale factor (`transform: scale(1.02)`) and an internal luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Glass Slate):** Translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`), `#FFFFFF` text. Hover increases fill opacity to `0.12`.
- **Destructive:** Subdued crimson tint (`rgba(255, 77, 77, 0.1)`), borders at `rgba(255, 77, 77, 0.25)`, text `#FF6B6B`.

### Bento Metric Cards
- Constructed with a `1.5rem` border-radius, `rgba(16, 18, 23, 0.8)` backdrop blur, and 1px crisp outline.
- Must feature a header row containing a subdued uppercase mono label (`label-mono-sm`) on the left and an optional status pill badge on the right.
- Numerical metric uses `headline-lg` in `Space Grotesk` or `JetBrains Mono`, paired directly below with a fractional percentage chip indicating delta velocity.

### Chips & Status Pills
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with a height of `24px` to `28px`.
- Composed of an ultra-subtle color wash (`rgba(color, 0.12)`), a 1px border (`rgba(color, 0.25)`), and contrasting vibrant text.
- Optional live pulse indicator: A 6px glowing dot with CSS ping animation positioned before the text label.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: Surface Elevation 2 (`#181B22`).
- 1px hairline border at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, transitioning cleanly to `#CCFF00` on focus.
- Monospaced numeric characters for transaction amounts, paired with absolute-positioned ticker labels (e.g., `USD`, `ETH`) pinned right.
- Error states swap border and hint labels to neon-coral (`#FF4D4D`).

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Toggle Switch:** Pill-shaped track (`44px x 24px`) in dark slate (`#181B22`) with a sliding circular thumb (`18px`). When active, track fills with `#CCFF00` and thumb shifts to `#08090C`.
- **Checkbox:** Compact rounded square (`rounded-md`, 20px x 20px), translucent background, with a vibrant `#CCFF00` check icon on selection.

### Telemetry Lists & Ledger Rows
- Minimalist stacked records separated by hairline dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
- Left-aligned icon container using frosted glass geometry, middle section for counterparty description (`Manrope` bold) and category tag (`JetBrains Mono`), right-aligned for value deltas with positive figures in `#00E599` and outflows in `#FFFFFF`.

### Micro-Interactions
- Tactile press feedback on interactive surfaces (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- Smooth spring curves for layout expands and drawer pulls (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
